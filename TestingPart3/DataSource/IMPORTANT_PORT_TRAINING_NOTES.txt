PORT-DOMAIN HEAD-ONLY EWASR FINE-TUNING — IMPORTANT NOTES
==========================================================

Purpose
-------
This is a separate first-iteration port-domain experiment for camera imagery
where the ASV is not visible in the frame. It adapts the predefined non-IMU
eWaSR MLX checkpoint to three-class port imagery.

Label convention
----------------
0 = obstacle
1 = water
2 = sky

The SAM3 masks are the target labels for training and the configured labels
for validation/test selection in this pseudo-label study. They should be
described as SAM3-derived pseudo-labels in the report. A final performance
claim still needs comparison with independent, manually annotated ground truth
masks, because agreement with SAM3 alone measures teacher agreement.

Data split (fixed by source recording/dataset)
----------------------------------------------
Train: 98 MODS kope104 image/mask pairs
  Images: data/mods/sequences/kope104-00065550-00066030/frames
  Masks:  TestingPart3/SAM3_Pseudo_Labels_Generator/output/MODS_Port_5ppt/masks

Validation: 35 LaRS Port image/mask pairs
  9  pairs from data/lars_v1-2/val/images/Port
  23 pairs from data/lars_v1-2/test/images/Port
  3  pairs from data/lars_v1-2/train/images/Port
  Masks for all three: TestingPart3/SAM3_Pseudo_Labels_Generator/output/LaRS_Port_5ppt/masks

Test: 20 Pohang Port image/mask pairs
  Images: data/pohang00/pohang00_port/left_images
  Masks:  TestingPart3/SAM3_Pseudo_Labels_Generator/output/Pohang_Port_5ppt/masks

The Pohang image folder holds 300 images but only 20 matching SAM3 masks. The
test split therefore contains every available labelled pair, not every raw
image. There is no random split of frames, and the roles use distinct source
recordings/datasets.

Method
------
Checkpoint: predefined non-IMU eWaSR ResNet-18 MLX weights
Trainable parameters: decoder.seg_head only
Frozen: ResNet-18 encoder, MetaFormer refinement modules, SIM fusion modules,
all other decoder modules, and BatchNorm affine/statistical state outside the
segmentation head.

Input: 512 x 384 RGB, bilinear resized and ImageNet normalised
Mask target: 128 x 96, nearest-neighbour resized
Loss: standard three-class cross-entropy
Optimizer: AdamW, weight decay 1e-4
Batch size: 2
Epochs per trial: 10
Learning-rate trials: 3e-4, 1e-3, and 3e-3
Selection: choose the trial/epoch with highest validation three-class mean IoU
Test: evaluate the selected model once on the held-out Pohang split

Run commands
------------
From TestingPart3/eWaSR_Head_Fine_Tuning:

  ../../TestingPart1/eWaSR/.venv/bin/python run_experiment.py \
    --config ../DataSource/head_only_port_v1.yaml --check-data

  ../../TestingPart1/eWaSR/.venv/bin/python run_experiment.py \
    --config ../DataSource/head_only_port_v1.yaml

Outputs are written below:
  TestingPart3/eWaSR_Head_Fine_Tuning/outputs/port_head_only_v1

Important study rule
--------------------
Do not choose a learning rate, number of epochs, or other hyperparameter using
the Pohang test result. Use LaRS validation for that choice, then preserve the
Pohang result for the single held-out pseudo-label evaluation. Afterwards,
compare the pretrained and selected fine-tuned model against independent human
ground truth for the final claim of performance improvement.

Observed first-run result (22 September 2026)
--------------------------------------------
All three configured learning-rate trials completed. The validation selection
was learning rate 1e-3 at epoch 8, with validation three-class mean IoU
0.844913 (pretrained baseline: 0.833089).

On the untouched 20-pair Pohang pseudo-label test split:
  Pretrained three-class mean IoU: 0.935191
  Adapted three-class mean IoU:    0.955051
  Change:                         +0.019860
  Pretrained pixel accuracy:       0.979801
  Adapted pixel accuracy:          0.985807
  Change:                         +0.006006

Water IoU changed from 0.992730 to 0.990471 (-0.002259), while obstacle and
sky IoU increased. These figures document agreement with the configured SAM3
labels only; they are not the final independently-ground-truth result.
